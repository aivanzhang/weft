    What got tracked?

    Each mark is one analyzed frame: tall marks are usable estimates, short marks are uncertain, and crosses are unusable. Unanalyzed frames stay blank. Visibility is not proof of correct anatomy.

    Supported: fixed-camera colored rigid objects in 2D, or the explicit rolling-ball profile. Not arbitrary-video understanding, 3D recovery, or proof of physical transfer.

    One world. Shared physics.

    Objects share coordinates, time, and contacts. Dimensions use image widths, not calibrated meters.

    Source-derived joint poses, projected into the simplified body. This is kinematic replay, not a learned action sequence.
