## Primary-source findings

### OpenBiomechanics: relevant measurements, incompatible current reuse terms

The [official project](https://openbiomechanics.org/) describes synchronized
genlock video and optical/force-plate data. The
[data license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drivelineresearch/openbiomechanics/main/LICENSE-DATA.md)
explicitly covers data and biomechanics documentation under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
with additional organizational exclusions and a commercial-license route.
Software is separately MIT. The publisher's terms therefore do not establish
permission for this product's use. No contact or purchase was attempted.

The [computer-vision README](https://github.com/drivelineresearch/openbiomechanics/blob/main/computer_vision/README.md)
was opened, but the returned extract did not identify a qualified small unit.
Exact clip duration/bytes, matched camera/floor sidecars and independent equipment
measurements remain **unknown**. Data rights must not be inferred from code MIT
or public GitHub availability.

### AddBiomechanics: independent forces, no verified camera-video join

The [official release page](https://addbiomechanics.org/download_data.html) states
dataset CC BY 4.0 and distinguishes optical marker locations and measured ground
forces/moments from estimated joints, torques and musculoskeletal properties.
That distinction makes the raw measurement route useful in principle. The page
lists the aggregate `http://archive.simtk.org/addbiomechanics/addbiomechanics.zip`;
the archive was **not requested**. Its exact bytes and member inventory are
unverified, and no bounded complete RGB trial was identified.

Missing: matched original RGB, intrinsic/extrinsic calibration, camera-to-floor
or force-plate transform and uncertainty, exact clock relation, and equipment
evidence. A measured ground force is not automatically a per-hand contact label;
processed skeletons are not independent pose truth. This is a promising licensed
measurement source, **not** yet a qualified video-reconstruction benchmark.

### GroundLink: paired motion/force, separate data terms still unknown

The [official implementation](https://github.com/hanxingjian/GroundLink) describes
paired motion and ground-reaction-force/center-of-pressure data and links to
separate data hosting at `csr.bu.edu`. It explicitly identifies MoSh++ pose/shape
as reconstructed. The repository's MIT statement was not transferred to the
separately hosted recordings or SMPL-X assets. No external download page/model
was requested. Raw video, numeric camera/floor calibration, timing, data rights,
individual duration/size and equipment reference remain **unverified**.

Two other search-only results did not repair this join: the
[prehabilitation radar release](https://zenodo.org/records/19829884) describes a
21.7-GB archive and model-derived hip-relative pose, not independent optical
ground truth; the
[consumer-IMU/force study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07183-6)
describes measured forces under CC BY 4.0 but no matched RGB calibration package.
Neither page was separately opened or acquired; no usage agreement was accepted.
