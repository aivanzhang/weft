# Independent analytic video fixtures

These eight small clips were generated by Weft's `ball_benchmark.create_analytic_probe` using closed-form equations rather than the simulator being tested. Source provenance, generation time, video hash, tracking configuration and analytic coordinates are preserved per directory. They are authored synthetic software tests, **not physical recordings**.

The generator is reproducible with the pinned local environment; encoded byte hashes may differ with another video codec/runtime. Do not replace a pinned source silently. `truth.json` is for scoring after reconstruction, never construction input.

See [the suite](../../benchmarks/README.md) and [measured ball results](https://github.com/aivanzhang/weft/blob/d0338eb926ef97ba05d59418ade028461ccbc9ae/docs/ball-diversity.md).
