## Reusable decision and next dependency

Use body/part-protected edge expansion as a cheap prerequisite screen. Require one connected disk, a simple boundary, valid vertex links and healthy remaining-body attachment before trying a constrained replacement. Reject failures rather than automatically increasing the radius or projecting a fan across a pinch.

For this source, the next specific dependency is **bounded body-side fan/attachment resolution at the three loop-27 junctions**, with explicit ownership of the listed original/cap faces. No whole-mesh remesh is justified. Two independently bounded loop-8/28 replacements could be studied separately, but their clean boundary result does not resolve the largest defect or authorize production acceptance.

Exact next-turn selections are in `boundary-attachment.json`: `source_face_ids` contains `[primitive, triangle]` pairs, `reference_body_source_faces` gives the immediate remaining-body neighborhood, and ordered boundary positions are included. The order is a deterministic undirected cycle, not an authored winding. No new triangles, smoothing, UV changes or skin transfer have been proposed as completed work.

Passing this topological screen would still not prove a good curved fit, nonintersection, visible quality or motion. The old cap/source intersection limitations remain. This screen did not test interior attachment ownership, perform a replacement, or infer hidden geometry.
