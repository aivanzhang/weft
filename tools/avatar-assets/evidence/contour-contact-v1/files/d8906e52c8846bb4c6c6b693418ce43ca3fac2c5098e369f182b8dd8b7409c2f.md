# Portable fringe profile diagnosis

Status: measured actual source, proposed geometry response; no new model or render.

The matched 720px EEVEE front and three-quarter previews show regular contour bristles and a comparatively smooth dark coat. The parent’s separate fringe-hidden ablation attributes the bristles to fringe geometry; contact/cap defects remain a separate workstream.

## Deciding measurements

- All 3,600 fins are straight, 8.000 mm long and 10.008 degrees from the source normal. Each cutout has exactly two straight alpha columns, 5 mm apart. Median column width halfway up is 1.271 mm; median opaque height is 7.714 mm. The width at about 80% height remains 57% of that near 20% height. This is a long narrow blade profile, not a bent or layered tuft.
- Only 15.54% of a median fin rectangle is above the actual alpha cutoff. Median nearest-root distance is 12.58 mm (3D chord, not geodesic). Roots represent 1.770 m² of eligible surface: 2,034 roots/m². These are allocation/area measurements, not visible coat coverage.
- In the recovered front camera, contour-oriented fins have median projected height 3.965 px at 720, but a half-height strip width of only 0.455 px. At 256 those become 1.410 px and 0.162 px. The full set’s median strip width is 0.553 px at 720 and 0.197 px at 256. Thus the contour can be tall enough to see while each blade is subpixel and filter-sensitive.
- Dark-source fins are not simply absent: 1,773 roots have linear luminance below .05, versus 1,804 above .5. Dark median nearest-any-root distance is 11.87 mm, compared with bright 13.14 mm. This does not prove equal area-normalized allocation, but does not support a blanket “too few dark roots” diagnosis. Median dark source luminance is .00478 versus bright .90461, so lighting contrast differs substantially.
- Opposite triangle windings already provide a front-facing side from either viewing direction; there is no whole-fin backface-culling loss except edge-on zero projected area. Source-surface shading normals, alpha/material policy, color and skin are present. GPU mip selection, depth occlusion, lighting and actual visible pixel coverage were not measured here.

## Smallest candidate: preserve roots, use the shared regional field

Propose a new opt-in post-selection response, not a changed v1 default. Keep all 3,600 roots, normals, source UV/color atlas, masks, materials, body geometry, skin and topology. At each exact root midpoint, evaluate the existing groom_flow direction, comb and length_scale. Set portable fin length to 4.5 mm × length_scale and orient the tip displacement with that field’s tangential comb. Existing selection stays frozen; no species coordinates or color-based special case is added.

The pinned field gives lengths 1.35–6.30 mm (median 4.50) and outward angles 18.43–61.70 degrees (median 55.64). Fixed-root projection predicts:

The reduction is not a density improvement: total unoccluded projected alpha area drops about half, and broad front-facing dark regions drop to .610×. This first candidate tests contour softness and restores meaningful regional direction/length; it cannot be claimed to solve sparse dark fur or Muse parity. Review both 720 and 256 appearances and preserve rejection if it reads thinner/bald. Do not hide contact defects under the coat.

Counts stay 76,981 triangles and the existing conservative 98,351-vertex ceiling; no texture/density/memory-cap increase is proposed. A pure reshaping helper has not yet been implemented by this receipt. Subsequent implementation/tests must remain separate artifacts.

If the contour-only candidate remains sparse, the next distinct hypothesis is a versioned, short connected tuft alpha profile with resolvable clustered bases and differentiated tips, preserving exact source-color sampling. That is not implemented, not authorized by this measurement, and must not be called a quality improvement without a controlled preview. Repeating more identical narrow blades is not the proposed remedy.

## Evidence and limits

`prepared.json` pins 12 actual inputs and seven source snapshots before the one CPU worker. `diagnosis.json` has per-view/per-luminance distributions and the exact candidate rules. `job.json` and `diagnose.log` retain the actual bounded run: 0.522 s, 199,114,752 B sampled direct-child RSS; worker’s terminal self sample was 221,249,536 B. The input hashes remain unchanged. No native, provider or AI-role calls; no running job. The worker did not execute any blend or edit a model/image.

Projection uses the frozen exporter preview’s orthographic mesh-bound-box fit, including its 1.18 padding, and reproduces saved bounds within 2e-7 m. It sums per-fin unoccluded alpha area, not rendered fur coverage. 256px is an analytical projection at the same camera, not a newly rendered image. Source-color bins use nearest stored atlas texels at the two nominal half-height tips; they are descriptive and do not alter selection. Alpha occupancy uses actual 60×28 cells with cutoff .18. Camera, mask and luma thresholds were frozen before execution.
