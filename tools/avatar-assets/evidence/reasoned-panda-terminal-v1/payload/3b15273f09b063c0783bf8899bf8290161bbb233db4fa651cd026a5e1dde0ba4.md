# Exported body-weight boundary inspection

Read-only measurements, not a causal appearance test. No provider/native calls or runtime edits.

Run: `.venv/bin/python outputs/d8-rig-weight-diagnosis-v1/measure.py` (already completed: 0.193 seconds internal, 173,326,336-byte peak RSS; 55-second CPU bound, observed below 512 MiB).

The constraint is not a hard step: `scripts/blender_finish_generated.py:188` uses `clip((1-distance)/.25, 0, 1)`: full weight within 75% of ellipsoid radius, linear fade to zero at 100%. Removed weight goes to the chosen fallback bone. Top-four selection then renormalizes. Narrow regions make the physical transition narrower; top-four selection can create jumps. Constraint schema offers center/radius, no configurable feather. The separate authored-rig layer schema does offer feather and smoothstep; all saved layer feathers are nonzero (0.05–0.18). Its inverse-fourth-power nearest-segment blend can be steep even before constraints.

Exact-position welds remove export/UV splits before measuring triangle-connected geometric edges. Both models have 18,071 unique positions and 54,197 welded edges; their body positions/topology match exactly. None of the 8,972 duplicate-position vertices disagrees in skin weights. This is not a UV-seam weight mismatch.

Across real edges, L1 total weight change (range 0–2) exceeds 1 on 183 model-0 edges versus 148 model-1 edges; p99 is 0.755 versus 0.702. Model-1's steepest transition changes from 86.3% upperarm_r to 98.6% forearm_r over one mesh-local edge of 0.0686. Large neighboring changes exist, but globally model-1 improves these statistics; evidence does not support blaming its narrower constraint alone. Max and largest-edge rows are saved in result.json.

The original source has no skin weights. No before-constraint weights are available in these exports, so these numbers cannot separate initial authored weighting, constraints, truncation, animation geometry or shading effects. They do not establish the cause of visible cuboid patches or justify weakening a gate. A future bounded pure-math replay of pre/post constraints, aligned to saved native bounds, could isolate the constraint contribution; first use the independent body-only visual ablation already assigned by root.
