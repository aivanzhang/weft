META GLOBAL AI DEVELOPER HACKATHON

Come and build the future of\
superintelligence.
=============================

Ten days, access to our newest models and \$1M in prizes. The Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon is virtual, worldwide and free to enter if selected.

[Apply now](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon)

## About the Hackathon

Apply to spend ten days building with Meta's newest models. Use the Muse Spark series, Muse Image and SAM 3.1 through Meta Model API, run Meta open weights models, and code faster with Muse Code agentic coding agent.Bring a problem you know firsthand, or an idea that feels out of reach. Build it with the models, learn from the Meta team and partners in live workshops, and compete for a share in the \$1,000,000 cash prize pool across two tracks: one for indie developers and one for startups.

## Participant Tracks

\$1,000,000 in total prizes. Every project enters one track.

TRACK 1

##### Indie developer track

For individual builders and students. Judged on creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works.

TRACK 2

##### Startup track

For teams and founders building a real product. Judged on technical execution and whether it holds up in production.

## Project Themes

Regardless of the track you choose, we’re looking for projects in two main themes:

THEME 1

##### Build for Your World

Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before.

THEME 2

##### Push the Frontier

Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, accelerate a scientific experiment, or unlock a better way to understand and control a physical system. Show us what becomes possible when these models meet the real world.

## Apply Now

Apply to participate. Participation is limited to a maximum of 20,000 accepted applicants. Applications will be evaluated against the selection criteria below on a rolling basis. No further applications will be accepted or evaluated once the cap is reached. Organizers reserve the right, in their sole discretion, to close the application portal and end the application period immediately once the participant cap is satisfied, even if such date is prior to the posted Application Deadline. Early application is encouraged.[Apply now](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon)

## How Applications are Reviewed

We aim for a mix of technical backgrounds and domains across participants. Applicants should best showcase their technical skills in the application form.We review every application against two criteria, using only your answers on the application form:

1.  Your project idea: how clear and feasible your proposed project is, and how well it fits one of the two project themes.
2.  Your technical skill: the technical skill shown in the AI project you describe, including what you built and the techniques you used. Links to your GitHub, LinkedIn or a shipped product help us verify your experience and skill but are not required.

Both criteria are weighted equally. Applications are evaluated on a rolling basis. Eligible applications meeting minimum scoring cut-offs will be accepted automatically, up to the first 20,000 acceptances. Eligible applications that do not meet the minimum scoring cut-offs will be accepted based on score received, if space allows. Answers to questions 13 to 16 help us improve our developer programs and are not used in selection.
