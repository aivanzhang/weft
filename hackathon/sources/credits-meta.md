Meta Global AI Hackathon: Model API Credit Claims

Congrats on being accepted to the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon! Every accepted participant can claim \$150 in Meta Model API credits to build with during the hackathon.

How to claim (about 5 minutes)

3.  Submit this form by Wed Nov 4, 2026 at 9:00 PM ET. Credits are added to your account within 2 business days. Each accepted participant can claim once, for their own account.
