## The multimodal intelligence of Muse Spark

Muse Spark treats what it perceives as something to act on, not just something to caption. This session explores what that shift unlocks for developers — across code, physical action, and video-heavy workflows — plus Muse Voice Transcribe, our first real-time audio perception model.
