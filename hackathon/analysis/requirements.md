## Terms that affect project decisions

- Participants retain ownership of their submissions, but the rules grant organizers broad, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, nonexclusive rights to use, modify, distribute, and otherwise exploit submissions. Do not interpret “you retain IP” as confidentiality or limited promotional use.
- Event materials are not treated as confidential. Keep proprietary employer/customer material out of the demo and public repository unless rights are established.
- The guide says repositories must be public. It does not name a required software license. Public visibility and an explicit open-source license are different decisions.
- Model API use requires a payment method. The credit terms say claiming/using credits is optional and participation does not require payment; open-weight models are another path.
- The PDF says credits are applied before other payment methods; further usage is charged after credits run out. A notification is not documented proof of a hard spend cap. Use application-side budgets if implementing API calls.
- No credit claim, card entry, agreement acceptance, public posting, or project submission was performed.
